      What is shared, and what stays personal?
      Shared: VET and TAS task status, checklists, overall review ticks, task record notes, assignments, triggered work, weekly checks and adjusted TAS dates. Source-linked VET/TAS cards include their progress and plain-text working notes.
      Personal: email imports and chains, unlinked notes, cards moved into Personal, daily pins, routines, Finance, appearance and private link settings. Working-note formatting and attachments are not transferred.
      Names identify a handover; they are not verified sign-ins. The file is not encrypted. Keep it in the restricted staff folder and keep official evidence in its authorised system.
      There is no live sync or remote editing lock. Let your colleague know when you are editing. Older and recognisably conflicting versions are blocked, but the website cannot prove a file is the latest or detect every competing history when versions are skipped.
