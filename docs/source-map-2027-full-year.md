# 2027 full-year source map and operating contract

## Identity and scope

- Product: WWHS VET Compliance Workboard — annual rollover and the complete 2027 school-year operating cycle.
- Audience: Head Teacher VET, VET Coordinator, VET Coordinator Assistant, Principal/authorised delegate and nominated supporting roles.
- Purpose: a privacy-safe staff workflow that presents the next dependency-cleared compliance action, shows future work without permitting early closure, and points staff to authorised owner systems.
- Explicit non-purpose: this is not a student course, assessment instrument, assessor guide, RTO record system, shared team database, evidence repository or proof of competency/compliance.
- Stable route: `#cycle-2027`; the earlier `#term1-2027` bookmark remains a compatibility alias to the same full-year cycle.
- Publication authority: Steve authorised release only after the expanded candidate passes fresh content, visual and operational acceptance. Operational activation remains a separate school-authority decision.

## Safe annual sequence

1. Preserve the 2026 audit trail and open clean 2027 instances.
2. Verify 2027 sources, calendar, delegation, access, delivery authority, trainer/assessor readiness and controlled resources.
3. Activate Term 1 only after the source/readiness gates are independently verified.
4. Run a fresh RTO-update control, focused operational work and independently verified close for every school week.
5. Close each term with a privacy-safe assurance summary, owned exceptions and an accepted hand-off to the next term.
6. Use the firm 30 June Stage 6 entry cut-off; all other unpublished NESA/RTO/WWHS dates remain current-source gates.
7. Run HSC entry, estimates, progressive outcomes and completion work only against the published 2027 actions and controlled records.
8. Close Term 4 through the final student day, 20 December, then preserve the annual trail and hand genuine carry-overs into a clean 2028 source-verification process.

## Recurring and interrupt controls

- Every week has a separate RTO notice occurrence and separate weekly closure record.
- Each term rechecks source versions, local calendar, roles/deputies and changed delivery state before term-specific work proceeds.
- Evidence/feedback assurance, cross-system reconciliation, trainer currency, SBAT monitoring, workplace-learning controls, privacy, validation and improvement recur at risk-appropriate points.
- Safety/privacy incidents, delivery/enrolment changes, support/RPL, handovers, discrepancies and validation/improvement requests create fresh occurrences and can interrupt the planned sequence.
- A workplace-learning occurrence follows the current relative procedure rather than a guessed school-week date.
- Waiting and exception work must have a role/system owner and chase date; escalation-due work re-enters the priority queue.
- Weekly, term and annual assurance closes require a different authorised verifier.

## Technical and privacy contract

- True dependencies and earlier-gate locks control both the guided queue and completion form.
- Future work remains visible, but verified and not-applicable closure is rejected before its operating window.
- Not-applicable closure requires current-source confirmation, authority reference, privacy-safe reason and authorised verifier; independent controls also require a different person.
- Hand-offs move only through saved transitions: none → sent → accepted/returned → verified.
- Import/export accepts the prior Term 1 prototype and 2026 schema-3 build without discarding valid 2027 Term 1 progress or importing authenticated links.
- Imported/exported state is explicitly allowlisted so unknown/private fields cannot be carried through a backup.
- Stable navigation, keyboard operation, visible focus, print safety and exact 390 CSS pixel containment apply to Setup, T1 W1–10, T2 W1–10, T3 W1–10 and T4 W1–11.

## Visual scope

The qualifying-photograph target remains **0**. This is a staff operations workboard, not a student learning package. Photographs would be decorative, increase privacy/rights risk and weaken the one-action interface. Visual acceptance therefore tests hierarchy, status clarity, responsive containment, touch targets, focus, contrast and print—not photo quantity.

## Activation gaps and invalidation

Release may occur after formal local acceptance because the public site is explicitly a privacy-safe workflow/launch layer. Actual 2027 operational activation remains gated until:

- the 2027 NESA Timetable of Actions and HSC key dates are published and recorded;
- the current authenticated RTO 90333 term guidance/notices are verified;
- the complete WWHS/Sentral staff calendar is reconciled;
- formal Principal role, deputy, access, escalation and independent-verifier delegation is recorded;
- a school-approved shared team register is connected or the device-local limitation is formally accepted; and
- signed-in staff test every work-account front door and sharing boundary.

Invalidate and re-audit the affected controls whenever a controlling source changes the sequence, date, role, evidence destination or escalation model.
